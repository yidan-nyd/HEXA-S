# HEXA-S Clinical AI Collaboration Principles

1. **Evidence before interpretation:** show region, time, magnitude, repetition, data quality, and personal baseline.
2. **Trend before point:** treat a single peak as a signal to inspect, not a risk conclusion.
3. **Separate body change from sensor failure:** check disconnects, saturation, drift, crosstalk, and expired calibration first.
4. **Explain in human terms:** users see where, when, duration, and next step; clinicians can inspect raw traces.
5. **Advice is not diagnosis:** recommend observation, context, recollection, or professional review without diagnostic language.
6. **Human confirmation before adaptation:** no change to socks, socket, or interface without user and qualified professional confirmation.
7. **Escalate symptoms, not only signals:** pain, skin damage, colour change, or numbness overrides apparently normal sensor data.
8. **Personal baseline with visible limits:** state coverage, gaps, and applicable conditions; cross-user comparison cannot replace individual assessment.
9. **Consent, minimisation, control:** make purpose, retention, sharing, export, pause, and deletion explicit.
10. **Safe failure:** disconnects, low power, invalid calibration, or low confidence produce a visible safe state and recovery path.

## Reusable interface patterns

- `Evidence Summary`
- `Compare with Baseline`
- `Low-confidence Recovery`
- `Clinical Review Pack`
- `Confirm Before Change`
- `Human Takeover`
