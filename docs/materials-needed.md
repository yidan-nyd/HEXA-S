# HEXA-S Evidence and Publishing Checklist

## Portfolio priorities

- Authentic hero image showing wearer, textile, electronics, and 3D model.
- 10–15 second end-to-end video of local stretch and mapped model response.
- Prototype evolution: one sensor → two → six-sensor ring → zig-zag network.
- One-to-one map of sensing point, body region, channel, and 3D object.
- Real raw, filtered, and normalised data comparison.
- TPU lattice samples with material-property differences.
- Accurate team roles and individual contribution.

## GitHub priorities

- Root `LICENSE` and third-party attribution.
- Circuit diagram, wiring photos, and exact BOM.
- Minimal Blender example file.
- Anonymised sample serial data.
- Per-garment calibration template.
- Channel–body-region–object mapping template.
- Repeatability, hysteresis, drift, and crosstalk results.
- End-to-end GIF / video and known limitations.
- Firmware, Blender, and environment versions.

## Do not present as delivered without evidence

- Accurate residual-limb dimensional measurement.
- A clinical-grade digital twin.
- Health-risk diagnosis.
- Clinically validated fitting recommendations.
- Automatically generated and deployed socket / lattice structures.
- Long-term reliability or patient outcome claims.
